THE RULES ARE SIMPLE

How to play

  1. Start at the first article.Choose your own two articles or let WikiRace pick them.
  2. Click links to travel.Only links inside the article count. Search and browser navigation won't help.
  3. Reach the destination.Get there with the lowest time and fewest clicks you can.
